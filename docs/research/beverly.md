## C — Identity notes

- **Nicknames/claims:** "The Garden City." (1) Birthplace of the American Navy — schooner Hannah, Glover's Wharf, commissioned Sept 5 1775; Hannah School at 42.5673,-70.8684; Marblehead disputes — THE rivalry fact, keep it playful. (2) Birthplace of America's Industrial Revolution — Beverly Cotton Manufactory 1787–88, first US cotton mill, Washington visited 1789; site ≈42.583,-70.881 (Dodge St). (3) Beverly Hills CA named after Beverly Farms (Burton Green, 1907); the "because Taft vacationed there" add-on is chronologically wrong (Taft first summered 1909).
- **The Farms vs the rest:** Beverly Farms (own zip 01965) tried to secede 3× (1886–88). Horribles Parade (July 4, roots 1888) — reference as "a famously silly Fourth of July parade," skip controversies. Neighborhoods: Ryal Side (OSM spells "Rial Side"; locals write RYAL), North Beverly, Centerville, Montserrat, Beverly Cove, Prides Crossing, Beverly Farms, Gloucester Crossing. John Updike lived in the Farms. Pete Frates = modern hometown hero.
- **Cabot St corridor:** storefronts ≈ Bow/Pond St (42.545) → Beverly Common/Dane (42.5525); NR district = Cabot from Central to Federal/Church + Church to Dane. Rantoul St (one block west) = Depot, breweries, new 5-storey apartment wave ("Rantoul is all condos now" — local sore/fun topic). Montserrat College of Art is downtown, not in Montserrat.
- **Harbors/moorings (seasonal boats):** Glover Wharf Municipal Marina (1 Water St), Beverly Port Marina (43 Water St, rack barns), Jubilee YC 42.5400,-70.8789; moorings Tuck Point ↔ Veterans Bridge; Bass Haven YC 42.5543,-70.8868 + Bass River moorings to the Hall-Whitaker bridge; Danvers River off Obear Park. Misery anchorage. Wenham Lake: ZERO boats (reservoir).
- **Friday lights:** BHS Panthers, orange/black; historic home Hurd Stadium; new lit turf at BHS ~2024 ("Forti Field" appears); Thanksgiving vs SALEM = one of the state's oldest rivalries — cross-town hook.
- **r/beverlyma fact-check list:** Essex Bridge not a draw; Hall-Whitaker saga; Balch 1636/1679; West Beach stickers; Captain Dusty's origin; Hurd politics; "The Shoe"; Ryal spelling; Dane St Beach is in Lyons Park; Nick's Famous Roast Beef (139 Dodge St, since 1975, ≈42.5826,-70.8842) — roast beef culture is sacred; Wenham Lake is tap water.
- **Tone:** Beverly is NOT a witch town — it's the "we ENDED the witch hunt" town (John Hale). Let Salem keep the tourists.
