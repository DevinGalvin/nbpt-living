# Present-Day Newburyport — Research Dossier

*Researched 2026-06-11 for NBPT Living. Status notes verified 2025–2026. For a kids' game (ages 6–12) on an exact city map.*

## 1. DOWNTOWN & WATERFRONT

- **Market Square** — brick-and-cobblestone heart of downtown at State St/Merrimac St/Water St junction. Holiday tree lighting happens here; Firehouse Center anchors it.
- **State Street** — main commercial spine running uphill from Market Square. Landmarks: The Screening Room cinema (82 State St), Anchor Stone Deck Pizza (44 State St), Richdale corner (State & Pleasant).
- **Inn Street pedestrian mall** — car-free brick lane between Market Square and Pleasant St. THE kid hub: **Inn Street Playground (25 Inn St)**; **Inn Street Fountain** (1975 Stuart Dawson sculpture-fountain), restored July 2023, with interactive pop-jet spray and night up-lighting — classic summer cool-off spot. **Simply Sweet candy/ice cream at 12 Inn St** (since 2002) and **The Angry Donut at 42 Inn St** (brioche donuts, giant cinnamon-sugar "angry" donuts) are right on the mall.
- **Pleasant Street** — parallel shopping street: **Harbor Creamery ice cream, 39 Pleasant St**; **Richdale convenience store, 1 Pleasant St (corner of State)** — beloved old-school five-and-dime: penny candy, beach supplies, NBPT t-shirts, open 5:30am–11pm daily. A small **"The Ice Cream Shop" (62 State St)** scoop counter operates in the same block.
- **Water Street** — runs along the harbor toward the South End/Joppa; passes Custom House, Coast Guard Station, Rear Range Light, and the Tannery (50 Water St).
- **Waterfront / Market Landing Park** — central waterfront officially **Market Landing Park** ($6M expansion opened June 2024, design by Sasaki): lawns, sculpture plaza, **trellis swings, hammocks, Adirondack chairs**, waterfront bike path. Contains the **1,100-ft boardwalk** along the Merrimack, the **waterfront concert stage**, ~4.4 acres.
- **Somerby's Landing** — sculpture park at the west end of the boardwalk (rotating outdoor sculptures).
- **Harbormaster** — office at **27R Water St, behind the Custom House**; manages 850 ft of visitor floating docks, Cashman Park boat launch, moorings, Plum Island Point Beach.
- **Whale watch / cruises** — **Newburyport Whale Watch (Captain's Lady III, 106-ft, 149 passengers)** departs the boardwalk at **54 Merrimac St**, May–Oct, trips to Jeffreys Ledge; evening/fireworks cruises.
- **Iconic storefronts**: **Jabberwocky Bookshop — Tannery Marketplace, 50 Water St** (indie institution, big kids' section). **Henry Bear's Park toy store — Tannery, 50 Water St Ste 24** (open 2026). **The Dragon's Nest toy store (31 Market Square) CLOSED Aug 2025 after 37 years** — online only now. **Fowle's News (17 State St)** — famous art-deco neon "Fowle's" sign and facade preserved, but now **Brine Oyster Bar**.
- Bakeries: **Chococoa Baking Co. (Tannery)** — "The Whoopie" whoopie pies, ships nationwide. **Buttermilk Baking Co. (9 Liberty St) CLOSED 2025–26** — reopening as **"The Fresh Flour"** keeping legacy recipes. **Abraham's Bagels & Pizza, 11 Liberty St** — the OG local bagel stop.

## 2. MUSEUMS & CULTURE

- **Custom House Maritime Museum, 25 Water St** — 1835 granite custom house; clipper models, shipwreck artifacts; 50th anniversary 2025; free summer kids programming. Thu–Sat 10–5, Sun 12–5.
- **Museum of Old Newbury / Cushing House, 98 High St** — 1808 Federal mansion + garden; tours Thu–Sun, late May–mid Oct.
- **Firehouse Center for the Arts, 1 Market Square** — 1823 former firehouse turned theater; family musicals, concerts, youth theater.
- **Newburyport Public Library, 94 State St** — Children's Room, storytimes, big Summer Reading program. (Building = 1771 Tracy Mansion; Washington slept here.)
- **The Screening Room, 82 State St** — tiny beloved indie cinema.
- **Tannery Marketplace, 50 Water St** — mill complex; Jabberwocky Books, Henry Bear's Park toys, Chococoa Cafe, The Dance Place, **Sunday farmers market**.
- **Newburyport Rear Range Light, 61½ Water St** — 53-ft brick lighthouse tower standing downtown; Lighthouse Preservation Society runs famous private "dinner at the top" experiences (only one in the US).

## 3. PARKS & OUTDOORS

- **Market Landing/Waterfront Park** — boardwalk, swings, concerts (see above).
- **Cashman Park (Merrimac St riverfront)** — big fenced riverfront **playground**, tennis/basketball, ball fields, the city's **public boat ramp**. NO skate park here.
- **Skatepark — rear of Nock Middle School, 70 Low St** (since 2001).
- **Joppa Park (Water St, South End)** — narrow waterfront park, brick walkway (redone ~2023), small boat ramp, tidal-flat views toward Plum Island; on the rail trail.
- **Bartlet Mall (High St & Pond St)** — historic park with **Frog Pond** (glacial kettle pond), fountain, ducks, 1805 Superior Courthouse; **ice skating when ice allows**; Yankee Homecoming "Olde Fashioned Sunday" (pony rides, mini train around the pond) and Art on the Mall. (February "Winter Carnival" on indefinite hiatus — don't show as current.)
- **Atkinson Common (north end of High St at Moseley Ave)** — Victorian park (Belleville Improvement Society, since 1893); **1936 WPA stone observation tower**, playground, tennis, rose garden.
- **March's Hill (High St at Bromfield St)** — THE classic **sledding hill** after every snowstorm.
- **Moseley Woods (14 Spofford St)** — 16-acre riverside pine woods at the Chain Bridge: playground, picnic pavilion, trails.
- **Maudslay State Park (Curzon Mill Rd)** — 480-acre former estate: 16 mi of trails, mountain laurel, 19th-c. gardens; **Theater in the Open** (May "Rites of Spring" giant-puppet pageant; October "Maudslay is Haunted" walk; summer arts camps); **winter bald-eagle roost** (a section closes each winter); sledding/XC skiing.
- **Clipper City Rail Trail + Harborwalk** — paved **~3.35 mi loop completed 2024**; MBTA station → March's Hill → downtown → river (Harborwalk) → Joppa Park; public art all along; connects to the **Garrison Trail** (1.8-mi path over the I-95 Whittier Bridge — first bike path on an interstate bridge in MA).
- **Little River Trail System / Common Pasture** — 5.4-mi trail network off 95 Storey Ave (also Hale St, Colby Farm Rd lots); colonial-era farmland, birding.
- **Community gardens** — city plots via the Office of Community & Economic Development.

## 4. PLUM ISLAND & WILDLIFE

- **THE PINK HOUSE (Plum Island Turnpike, Newbury) — DEMOLISHED March 11, 2025** by US Fish & Wildlife after the long "Support the Pink House" fight. **April 2026: site memorialized with a sign between two granite posts** featuring a Kathy Culbert painting: "honoring 100 years of memories. Demolished March 11, 2025, but never forgotten." (1925–2025.) In-game: empty marsh lot + memorial in 2026 reality — strong nostalgia/mystery hook.
- **Plum Island Lighthouse (Newburyport Harbor Light), Plum Island Point** — 1788-lineage white wooden tower (current 1898); **free 15-min tours by Friends of Plum Island Light** ~6 Saturdays per summer; 5 people/slot, must be 5+ yrs and 42" tall.
- **Beaches** — Plum Island Point & North Point beach (Newburyport-managed, by the lighthouse/jetty), center-island Newbury town beach, refuge lots 1–7, **Sandy Point State Reservation** at the southern tip (fills by early morning in summer).
- **Parker River National Wildlife Refuge** (HQ/visitor center **6 Plum Island Tpke**, gatehouse on the island) — $5/car. **Piping plovers: most of the refuge beach closes April 1–early August** for nesting; refuge has the 2nd-largest plover population on the North Shore. **Hellcat Interpretive Boardwalk Trail** — rebuilt 2021, 1.3-mi accessible boardwalk through marsh/dunes. **Greenheads: biting flies peak last two weeks of July–first week of August**; only females bite; drawn to dark colors; windy days are safe — locals literally check greenheadreport.com. Surf fishing for striped bass is iconic.
- **Joppa Flats Education Center (Mass Audubon), 1 Plum Island Tpke** — family drop-in programs, birding classes, **summer day camp ages 6–14**, hosts the **Eagle Festival** and January's **Superbowl of Birding**.
- **Surfland Bait & Tackle (Plum Island center)** — legendary surf-fishing shop, daily fishing reports.
- **Plum Island Airport / Aerodrome (2B2), Plum Island Tpke, Newbury** — grass strip owned by Historic New England; New England's oldest flying field (1910 Burgess flights); flight school, scenic rides.

## 5. ANNUAL EVENTS

- **Yankee Homecoming** — 9 days, last Sat of July–first Sun of Aug. Free **nightly waterfront concerts**; **fireworks over the Merrimack** final Saturday; **Grand Parade down High Street** final Sunday noon; **Olde Fashioned Sunday at Bartlet Mall** (pony rides, mini train, sack races, pet show); **Kids Day in the Park** (decorated bike parade, face painting); **Kids Talent Show** (waterfront stage); **Lions Club Road Races Tuesday evening — 5K + famous 10-Miler** (start/finish at Newburyport High). No amusement-ride midway in the current era.
- **Santa's arrival + tree lighting** — Sunday after Thanksgiving: **Santa & Mrs. Claus arrive by US Coast Guard boat at the waterfront** (30+ year tradition), parade through downtown, **Market Square tree lighting ~5pm**. New 2025: month-long **"Port of Wonder"** holiday celebration.
- **Spring Fest** — Memorial Day weekend, downtown arts festival.
- **Fall Fest (Fall Harvest Festival)** — Columbus Day weekend, downtown.
- **Newburyport Farmers Market** — **Sundays 9am–1pm, May–Nov, at the Tannery**, rain or shine.
- **Merrimack River Eagle Festival** — mid-February (20th annual: Feb 14, 2026); raptor shows, eagle-viewing stations, van tours.
- **Newburyport Literary Festival** — late April; children's authors track.
- **PlumFest** — Plum Island "porch fest," mid-September; 100+ acts on porches/yards; shuttles from Plum Island Airport.
- Also: **Superbowl of Birding** (late January), Pride parade (Bartlet Mall to Brown Square, June), sidewalk sales during YH week.

## 6. KID LIFE

- **Schools (mascot: Clippers, crimson & gold)** — Bresnahan Elementary (PreK–3), **333 High St**; Molin Upper Elementary (4–5) + Nock Middle (6–8) share **70 Low St** (skatepark behind); Newburyport High (9–12), **241 High St** — World War Memorial Stadium.
- **Youth sailing** — **American Yacht Club Sailing School** (on the Merrimack near the river mouth): 60+ years of summer learn-to-sail, ages 9–17, Hunter 140 dinghies.
- **Hockey/skating** — **Henry Graf Jr. Memorial Rink, 28 Low St**: public skating, Newburyport Youth Hockey. Outdoor: Frog Pond.
- **Best playgrounds** — Inn Street (downtown), Cashman Park (riverfront), Moseley Woods (woodsy), Atkinson Common.
- **YWCA Greater Newburyport, 13 Market St** — two pools, swim lessons, afterschool, camps ages 3–16. (No YMCA in town.)
- **Newburyport Youth Services ("the Rec"), 40 Milk St** — 2,000+ kids/year, year-round programs.
- **Dance/arts** — The Dance Place (Tannery); Newburyport Aikido; Crane Karate; Firehouse youth theater.
- **Summer camps** — Theater in the Open (Maudslay, from age 5); Mass Audubon Joppa Flats (6–14); YWCA; NYS rec; AYC sailing.

## 8. TRANSPORT & MOTION

- **MBTA Commuter Rail — Newburyport Station, 25 Boston Way** (off the Route 1 traffic circle); terminus of the Newburyport/Rockport Line to Boston North Station; **rail trail starts at the platform**.
- **Gillis Memorial Bridge (Route 1 drawbridge over the Merrimack, built 1976)** — **in summer (Memorial Day–Labor Day, 6am–10pm) the draw opens on signal only on the hour and half hour** — traffic stops, boats parade through; occasionally gets stuck (news events). Full operations confirmed June 2026 after repairs. Perfect timed-event game mechanic.
- **Chain Bridge (Merrimac St/Bridge Rd via Deer Island to Amesbury)** — site of the first chain suspension bridge in America (1810; current 1910 structure); 200 yards from Moseley Woods.
- **Whittier Memorial Bridge (I-95)** — rebuilt 2013–2018; carries the **Garrison Trail** bike/walk path with river overlooks.
- **Buses** — **MeVa (Merrimack Valley Transit) is fare-free**: Route 19 Amesbury↔MBTA↔downtown↔Tannery; Route 20 MBTA↔downtown↔Salisbury Beach. No current seasonal trolley to Plum Island.
- **Kayak/SUP** — **Plum Island Kayak, 92 Merrimac St**: rentals + guided tours (full-moon, seal, refuge paddles).
- **Fishing charters/party boats** — **Captain's Fishing Parties, 10 82nd St, Plum Island Point**: deep-sea party boats + the whale watch fleet.
- **Bikes** — rail-trail culture is big; trail public art; connects MBTA→downtown→Harborwalk→Joppa.

## 9. LOCAL IDENTITY

- **"Clipper City"** — clipper ship on the city water tower; high-school teams are the **Clippers**; Clipper Heritage Trail; Clipper City Rail Trail.
- **City seal** — ship imagery; clipper silhouette is the de facto logo.
- **NBPT sticker culture** — "NBPT" oval stickers and "NBPT 01950" merch everywhere; Richdale sells thousands of NBPT tees.
- **Rivalry with Amesbury** — **Thanksgiving Day high-school football since 1891, 5th-oldest rivalry in Massachusetts**.
- **Famous faces** — **John Cena grew up in next-door West Newbury** (2025 Black Cow sightings); novelist Andre Dubus III lives locally; historic icons: Garrison, "Lord" Timothy Dexter.
- Coast Guard pride: birthplace of the USCG; Santa arrives on a Coast Guard boat.

2. **The Angry Donut (42 Inn St)** — collect/deliver giant "angry" donuts.

5. **Boardwalk + Market Landing Park swings/hammocks** — street performers, nightly summer concerts.

7. **Gillis drawbridge openings (Route 1)** — on the hour/half-hour in summer: time your crossing — built-in clock mechanic.
8. **Plover Patrol at Parker River refuge (Plum Island)** — protect roped-off nests April–August.

13. **March's Hill sledding (High St at Bromfield)** — snow-day race course.
14. **Frog Pond at Bartlet Mall** — ducks in summer, wild-ice skating in winter; pony/train rides during Yankee Homecoming.

16. **Santa arrives by Coast Guard boat → Market Square tree lighting** — winter event finale.
17. **Eagle Festival (Feb, Joppa Flats EC)** — winter bald-eagle spotting; Maudslay roost.
