# Gloucester + Rockport, MA — hero-building architecture dossier (July 13, 2026)

Compiled for the Gloucester town build (map frame includes Rockport); colors marked
PHOTO-VERIFIED were checked against actual photographs viewed in-browser (Wikimedia
Commons `thumb.php` renders, official/museum sites, HMdb, sculptor's own site) —
never prose alone. OSM names verified against a live Overpass pull (kumi mirror,
7/13/2026, data timestamp 2026-05-31). Watch the OSM quirks called out per entry —
Gloucester/Rockport OSM has several misspellings and name-on-node-not-way traps.

## A — Hero buildings & monuments

### The two must-nails

1. **Hammond Castle** (OSM building way/190857013 `Hammond Castle Museum`, addr 74
   Hesperus Ave — museum + Wikipedia say **80 Hesperus Ave**; also POI node/1408378021
   `Hammond Castle` and THREE park/grounds ways named `Hammond Castle Museum`
   29688926/29689914/29694229) — 1926–29, John Hays Hammond Jr. (the inventor) with
   Allen & Collens; medieval-pastiche castle on the shore rocks facing **Norman's Woe**
   reef. Final massing ~**81 ft high, ~142 × 70 ft footprint** (Wikipedia, from the
   build history). PHOTO-VERIFIED (Commons `Hammond castle front.jpg`,
   `Hammond Castle, Gloucester, Massachusetts 01.JPG` passage detail, museum-site
   courtyard photo, all viewed): walls = **rough mixed-tone granite fieldstone rubble**
   — gray with brown/rust/tan stones, pale tan mortar — with **cut-granite ashlar**
   around openings and in the gatehouse passage; entry = pointed Gothic arch in pale
   cut stone, dark wood door, **drawbridge deck with big iron CHAINS** angling up the
   wall, carved stone relief plaque above; buttressed great-hall wall with **tall
   pointed-arch Gothic windows**; big square stone TOWER with pyramidal **gray slate**
   cap + smaller round turret forms; sections of **buff/tan stucco with steep slate
   roofs** and (solarium court) **brown half-timber over stucco** on upper storeys;
   sea-side courtyard has a **gray-granite Gothic ARCADE** (pointed twin arches on
   colonnettes) right over the water; white stone lion by the entry; black iron
   lanterns. Interior fact for cards: Great Hall with rose window + 8,200-pipe organ
   (verified interior photo). Footprint: long irregular bar along the cliff, courtyard
   behind the entrance block. CONFIDENCE: **HIGH** (4 photos viewed; hammondcastle.org
   + Wikipedia for dates). Trap: don't make it uniform gray ashlar — the body is
   mixed-color rubble, and parts are stucco/half-timber.

2. **Our Lady of Good Voyage** (OSM way/55411558 — named `Our Lady of Good Voyages
   Church` — note the wrong extra "s" in OSM; addr 142 Prospect St) — current church
   **1915** (predecessor 1892/93 burned 1914), Mission/Spanish Revival modeled on the
   Santa Maria Madalena church in the Azores; the Portuguese fishermen's parish.
   PHOTO-VERIFIED (3 Commons photos, 2008 + July 2013 + side view, all viewed):
   **pale cream / light-tan STUCCO** walls, **dark chocolate-brown trim** (cornices,
   tower belts, window surrounds, curved gable copings); twin square front towers
   with open arched belfries topped by bulbous **BLUE domes with GOLD crosses** —
   dome shade reads **medium royal blue** in two photos, lighter sky-blue in the 2013
   one (repaint/lighting) — use royal blue, re-check a 2025-26 photo before final
   texture; between the towers on the scrolled center gable stands the white-robed
   **Madonna holding a SCHOONER in her left arm** (today's rooftop statue is a
   replica — Angelo Lualdi's 1915 painted-wood original is displayed at the Cape Ann
   Museum); big round quatrefoil rose window center, arched windows, **three BLUE
   doors** (ornate brown center portal with gilt shell), granite steps; long gable
   sanctuary behind with **pale gray shingle roof** and tall arched stained-glass
   windows between pilaster strips; cream Mission-parapet parish wings behind.
   Carillon (1922) — "one of the oldest full carillons in the US" (Wikipedia).
   Footprint: rectangle nave + twin-tower front + rear hall. CONFIDENCE: **HIGH**
   (3 photos; goodmorninggloucester.com + capeannmuseum for the statue-replica fact).

### Downtown / civic

3. **Gloucester City Hall** (OSM POI node/257584556 `Gloucester City Hall`,
   amenity=townhall, 9 Dale Ave — name is on the NODE; the building way is unnamed)
   — 1869–70, Gridley J.F. Bryant & Louis P. Rogers, Second Empire. PHOTO-VERIFIED
   (Commons `Gloucester City Hall - Gloucester, MA - DSC01511.jpg`): **red brick**
   body, 2 tall storeys over a granite raised basement, **cream/buff painted wood
   trim** — bracketed cornices, arched window surrounds; dark **gray slate** roofs;
   four corner pavilions with steep pyramidal slate roofs each topped by a small
   cream cupola; central front **CLOCK TOWER**: brick lower stages, then cream wood
   stages — white clock faces, open arched belfry with balustrades — crowned by a
   **dull-brown oxidized COPPER dome** (NOT verdigris green — trap) with weathervane;
   cream 4-column entry portico with balustrade, granite steps; small Civil War
   monument (green bronze on granite shaft) on the lawn; granite retaining wall.
   Footprint: rectangle w/ corner pavilions + tower bay. CONFIDENCE: **HIGH**.

4. **Gloucester Fisherman's Memorial / "Man at the Wheel"** (OSM node/358268633
   `Fishermens' Monument` (sic, apostrophe after the s), historic=memorial,
   memorial=statue, on South Stacy Blvd; GNIS twin node/1408381847 `The Fishermen's
   Memorial`) — commissioned for the 1923 tercentenary, **bronze cast 1925** (Leonard
   Craske, Gorham Co.). PHOTO-VERIFIED (Commons 2019 photo): **8-ft fisherman in
   sou'wester + oilskins, fully sea-GREEN verdigris patina** (statue AND wheel),
   braced wide-legged on a sloping deck, hands on a ship's wheel angled across his
   body, gazing out; base = **rough-faced light-gray granite block** with smooth
   panel incised **"THEY THAT GO DOWN TO THE SEA IN SHIPS 1623–1923"**; stands on a
   half-round cobble/sett plaza ringed by **short dark posts with hanging chain
   swags**, small green-copper dome marker at the front curb; he faces OUT over the
   harbor (inscription faces the boulevard). Bronze cenotaph plaques with the names
   of the ~5,300 lost are mounted along the adjacent walkway (MEDIUM detail). This is
   a MONUMENT — model statue + base + chain ring, not a building. CONFIDENCE: **HIGH**.

### Lighthouses & harbor works

11. **Eastern Point Light** (OSM way/1033568181 `Eastern Point Light`,
    man_made=lighthouse; station way/1033580529 `Eastern Point Light Station`; fog

    tower, BLACK lantern + gallery, RED conical lantern roof with red ball finial**.

12. **Dog Bar Breakwater** (OSM way/55169774 `Dog Bar Breakwater`,
    man_made=breakwater + path way/1213334799) — 1894–1905, Army Corps + Rockport
    Granite Co.: **2,250 ft** of dry-laid **cut gray granite blocks** (7 stepped
    tiers, ~31 ft wide on top) running from Eastern Point across the harbor mouth.
    PHOTO-VERIFIED (Commons): at the seaward tip stands the **Gloucester Breakwater
    Light (1905)** — a **white skeletal steel platform tower** carrying a tiny white
    house with **red-brown shingled gable roof** + light mast. Walkable; model the
    blocky top surface. CONFIDENCE: **HIGH**.

13. **Ten Pound Island Light** (OSM way/713682287 `Ten Pound Island Light`,
    building=lighthouse; ruins way/1447850200 `Coast Guard Aviation Station Ten Pound
    Island ruins`; there is also a memorial-stone NODE 9502675715 named "Ten Pound
    Island Lighthouse" on the boulevard — don't confuse) — current tower 1881:
    **39-ft WHITE conical CAST-IRON tower, BLACK lantern, gallery + domed cap**,
    solar panels on small frames beside it. PHOTO-VERIFIED (Commons morning photo).

14. **Annisquam Harbor Light** (OSM way/713444797 `Annisquam Harbor Light`, 45

15. **Thacher Island Twin Lights / Cape Ann Light Station** (OSM ways 713671588 +
    713671589, BOTH named `Cape Ann Light (Twin Lights)`, building=lighthouse; island
    way/81681752 `Thacher Island` + NWR ways) — 1861 twins, **124 ft tall, 900 ft
    apart**, N–S pair on a low scrubby island off Rockport. PHOTO-VERIFIED
    (`Thacher Island Lighthouses in 2009.jpg`): **UNPAINTED GRAY GRANITE conical
    towers** — mottled tan-gray with dark weather streaks, NOT white — with
    **pale-GREEN verdigris copper dome roofs**, dark lanterns + gallery rails; solar

    Operation (thacherisland.org): **south tower = active USCG aid, flashing RED;
    north tower = private aid relit 1989, steady AMBER** — the only operating twin
    light station in the US (great card). CONFIDENCE: **HIGH**.

## B — Model these first (iconic-ness × confidence)

1. **Hammond Castle** — the region's one true castle; drawbridge chains, mixed
   rubble stone, slate caps, sea-side Gothic arcade all verified. THE set piece.
2. **Motif #1** — "most-painted building in America"; exact weathered barn-red +
   gray roof + buoy wall verified; cheap two-volume build, giant payoff.
3. **Our Lady of Good Voyage** — twin blue onion domes + schooner Madonna; nothing
   else on the map looks like it; three photos triangulated.
4. **Man at the Wheel** — the city's symbol; green-patina statue + inscribed granite
   base + chain-ring plaza verified.
5. **Gloucester City Hall** — red brick + cream trim + slate corner pavilions +
   brown-copper-domed clock tower; skyline anchor over downtown.
6. **Eastern Point Light + Dog Bar Breakwater** — white/black/red-cap tower, white
   Queen Anne keeper's house, red-A-frame fog bell, then 2,250 ft of walkable granite
   ending in the little white skeletal breakwater light — a whole quest location.
7. **Paint Factory** — harbor-mouth icon; weathered red wood + white ghost
   MANUFACTORY lettering (decal!) + brick chimney on granite base.
8. **Thacher Island twins** — two 124-ft unpainted granite towers with green copper
   tops, 900 ft apart; unique in the US; simple cones, huge silhouette value.
9. **Fishermen's Wives Memorial** — pairs with #4 on Stacy Boulevard; boulder base +
   mother-and-children bronze verified.
10. **Beauport** — the 56-room shingle fantasy on Eastern Point; olive shingle, stone
    tower, chimney forest; medium-detail cluster reads instantly.
